Questions
Is ABTalks actually free?

Yes — joining and running the full 60-day challenge costs nothing. There's no card required to start Day 1.

What if I miss a day?

Your streak resets, but your longest streak stays on record, and every student gets a streak freeze to protect one missed day without losing progress.

Do I need to already know how to code?

You need the basics of your chosen track. Tasks ramp in difficulty across the 60 days rather than assuming expert-level skill on Day 1.

Why does the theme change as my streak grows?

It's a visible, honest signal of consistency — 20 named themes across the 60 days (Ash & Charcoal, First Spark, Emerald Forest, Cosmic Nebula… all the way to Supernova Gold), each unlocked every 3 days you build. Not a cosmetic skin — a real progress marker.
